Who We Partner With

Open to labels, agencies, events, media, and more

Every partnership is different. Here is what we bring to each type.

Labels

A pipeline of release-ready artists

We develop not-yet-signed artists to release level. You get first pick of emerging talent, and we share the commission on signings and releases.

Agencies

Talent you can sign to your roster

We train artists below your current bar. After the academy, you can sign them to your roster knowing they have the skills, brand, and work ethic to perform.

Events

Artists and DJ contests, co-created

We supply artists and co-create DJ contests for your events. DJ-pool referrals earn a commission, and our community fills your line-ups with fresh talent.

Media

Joint campaigns, shared audience

Joint campaigns on Instagram and your platforms, plus your network put to work for commission. We bring the content and artists, you bring the reach.

Educators

Complementary programs, not competition

If you teach music production, DJing, or music business, our academy adds structured career support. Refer students who want the next level.

Brands

Authentic access to the music community

Brands in audio gear, software, or lifestyle can co-create campaigns, sponsor events, and reach a highly engaged audience of active producers and DJs.
