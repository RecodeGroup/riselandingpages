The Problem

You see potential in some artists, but you can't develop every one of them yourself. Rejecting promising talent feels like leaving value on the table.

How Rise Solves This

Instead of rejecting artists, refer them to Rise. Earn a commission on every artist that joins Rise through your referral. With our affiliate model, referring 10 to 50 artists per month generates recurring revenue — while we handle all the development. You turn every rejection into a revenue opportunity and a future signing.

They complete 6 months of coaching, come back with polished tracks, and you sign the release. You earned a referral commission and got a release-ready artist.

Cross-referral model — we recommend your platform to our artists, you recommend Rise to your users. Affiliate commission flows both ways. Your platform gets educated users who actually engage (lower churn, higher LTV), and Rise artists get professional tools they know how to use.

Artists who come through Rise are significantly more engaged on your platform. Meanwhile, you refer your users to Rise for the education they need — earning commission on every signup.
