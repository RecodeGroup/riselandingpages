Who this is for

Made for artists ready to take serious steps.

Core is for producers ready to take real steps in the industry and put in the fight it takes to get there. It isn't an entry-level program, and it isn't for everyone. There's selection before you're in, and once you are, there's no coasting. We won't pat you on the back. We hold up a mirror instead.

The program

What a year in Core looks like.

  • You set a 90-day goal: 3 gigs or a festival booking.
  • You build the plan to get there, and we hold you to it every week.

Weekly group sessions

Live group sessions on your plan: are you on track, what worked, what didn't, and what you do next.

Your artist profile

Your positioning, story, visuals and press kit in place, so promoters and labels see an artist.

Feedback on releases and bookings

Targeted feedback on your tracks, the right labels or release route, and your outreach to promoters.

Writing camps and community

Remote Sessions with our labels, masterclasses, and a community that ends up on each other's line-ups.

Your year in Core

Four areas from producer to artist

You pick a 90-day goal and build your plan in The Industry Code. Then you work on these four areas, every week.

AREA 1 Direction & Profile

Where you stand, a realistic goal, and your positioning, story, visuals and press kit.

AREA 2 Music & Releases

A stock of finished tracks, the right labels or release route, and tracking what you send out.

AREA 3 Visibility

A content plan, posting consistently, set recordings and contact with your audience.

AREA 4 Network & Gigs

Researching promoters and labels, personal outreach, following up and going to events.
