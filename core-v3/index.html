Who this is for

Made for artists ready to take serious steps.

Core is for producers ready to take real steps in the industry and put in the fight it takes to get there. It isn't an entry-level program, and it isn't for everyone. There's selection before you're in, and once you are, there's no coasting. We won't pat you on the back. We hold up a mirror instead.

The program

What a year in Core looks like.

Weekly live sessions where you're held accountable, not just informed. Advanced lesson modules and platform content unlock throughout the year, including the modules and Course that aren't part of Base. The point isn't what you learn; it's whether you actually do it.
