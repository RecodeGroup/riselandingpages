Who it's for

Made for artists who are ready to make serious steps.

Core is for producers who are ready to take real steps in the industry and put in the work it takes to get there. It isn't an entry-level program, and it isn't for everyone. There's selection before you're in, and once you are, you're expected to show up every single week.

You'll be surrounded by artists chasing the same goal, pushing each other forward through feedback, accountability and shared ambition. We won't pat you on the back. We hold up a mirror instead.

The program

What a year in Core actually looks like.

Every week you'll join a live session with Maurits and the rest of the Core community. You'll share your progress, receive honest feedback and stay accountable to the goals you've set. Throughout the year, new lesson modules and platform content unlock as you grow.

The point isn't just what you learn, it's what you create and who you become while you're here.
