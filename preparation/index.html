Inside Base

30 lessons. Three finished tracks.

25 live production lessons of three hours each, built around three projects, plus five lessons on artist development. Every lesson moves one of your own tracks forward.

Foundations

Lessons 1–5
  • Your first production
  • Drums and groove
  • Notes, scales and basslines
  • Chords and harmony
  • Melody, hooks and riffs

Sound

Lessons 6–10
  • Sample selection and sound palette
  • Synthesis made simple
  • Sampling and audio manipulation
  • Creative effects and processing
  • Building a strong loop

Arrangement

Lessons 11–13
  • Arrangement fundamentals
  • Transitions, tension and energy
  • Automation, variation and ear candy

Mixing

Lessons 14–18
  • EQ and frequency balance
  • Compression, dynamics and sidechain
  • Reverb, delay and depth
  • Kick, bass and low end
  • Mixing a full track

Advanced

Lessons 19–25
  • Vocals and vocal production
  • Advanced groove and drums
  • Advanced sound design and resampling
  • Reference tracks
  • Workflow and finishing tracks
  • Full production session
  • Final mix, master review and next steps

Artist development

5 lessons
  • Who you are as an artist, and what you want to say with your music. The step from producer to artist starts here.
