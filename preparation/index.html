How Rise works

Built by artists who release and play internationally.

Rise is the artist academy of Recode Group, next to our record labels and our agency.

Base · 6 months

From a loop to a finished track.

Goal: three full, release-ready tracks within 6 months.
  • 30 live lessons in a small group: 25 on production, 5 on artist development
  • A weekly 3-hour live class, plus homework on your own tracks
  • Direct feedback on your music from coaches who release and play out
  • Expect around 8 hours a week: the class plus homework
From € 1.500,- · Payment options available
Core · 12 months

From producer to artist.

Goal: working toward 3 gigs or a festival booking within 90 days.
  • Your branding, goals and the concrete steps to get there
  • Group check-ins twice a week that hold you to your goals
  • Feedback on releases, bookings and your next steps
  • Remote Sessions writing camps with our labels and agencies, plus masterclasses and the community
From € 2.650,- · Payment options available

Results are goals we work toward together, not guarantees. They depend on the time and energy you put in.
