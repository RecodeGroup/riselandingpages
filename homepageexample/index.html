FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Rise.

What is Rise? +

Rise is an artist development platform for electronic music creators. It combines structured courses, expert coaching, and a community of committed producers into one system. Whether you want to sharpen your production skills or build a full artist career, Rise gives you the tools and the people to make it happen.

What is the difference between the Course and the Community? +

The Rise Course is the foundational program: 11 in-depth video modules covering production, branding, releasing, marketing and career development. The Community includes full Course access plus weekly accountability calls, monthly workshops, peer feedback and a private network of producers who are building real careers together. Within the Community there are three tiers: BASE, CORE and ELITE.

Do I get lifetime access? +

Yes. Once you purchase the Rise Course, you keep access forever. The video modules, sample packs, presets and templates are yours to use at your own pace. The community membership runs as long as you want to be part of it.

Is Rise right for me if I am a beginner? +

Absolutely. Rise is designed for all levels. Whether you are just starting out or you have been producing for years, the program meets you where you are. Beginners get a structured foundation; experienced producers get the strategy and network to take the next step.

What if it is not for me? +

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with the program, you get a full refund within the first 14 days. No questions asked.

How is the community structured? +

The community runs on a weekly rhythm: accountability calls where you set and review goals, monthly deep-dive workshops with industry experts, and ongoing peer feedback on your music and brand. Everything is designed to keep you moving forward consistently.

Who are the coaches? +

The Rise team consists of experienced producers, engineers, and entrepreneurs who are still active in the industry. They bring real-world expertise in production, mixing, mastering, branding, marketing, and business development.

Earn the Rise Certificate

After completing the Rise Course or Community program, you receive the official Rise Certificate, a credential recognized by agencies and event organizers. It proves you've built real skills and are ready for the next step. Rise is actively working with industry partners who verify against it, so your certificate opens doors from day one.
