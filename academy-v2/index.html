The idea

Turn your talent into a career.

What's missing isn't more information. It's direction, structure, and accountability that holds you to a higher standard than you'd hold yourself to.

You don't rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your standards.

The method

What happens once you're in.

Most producers already know they're stuck, they just don't know exactly where and how to fix it. So we start there, with an honest read on where your sound and your habits stand today, not where you assume they do.

From there it gets structural, not motivational. Deadlines you can't quietly slide past. A standard set higher than the one you'd hold yourself to. And progress measured only by what gets finished, posted and released, nothing else.

The programs

Base and Core, at a glance.

Base runs six months and builds the foundation, your sound, your workflow, your skill. Core runs a full year and turns that foundation into a career, with the same coaches, the same weekly sessions and the same standard throughout. Start the intake, and create the foundation you can build your career upon.
