The problem

Sound familiar?

You open a project, lay down a great idea, then get stuck at the same point. Arrangement, mixing, finishing — the gap between your loops and a real track feels impossible to cross alone.
YouTube, courses, masterclasses. You've tried them all. You learn something new, try it once, then forget it by next week. Knowledge without structure doesn't stick.

The program

What the six months include.

A new group starts every six months, and you can reserve your spot months ahead. Inside, it's 30 live sessions: most are hands-on production work, and a handful step outside production to sharpen your artist profile and the moves that push your career forward. Same six months, same group, same standard throughout.

30 live sessions

Weekly sessions with your coach, structured around production fundamentals, sound design, arrangement and mixing.

Small group model

You move through the program alongside a small group. You learn from each other, push each other, and finish together.

Direct feedback on your tracks

Submit your work, get honest feedback from your coach and group. No sugarcoating, just clarity on what works and what doesn't.

Career-focused sessions

A handful of sessions go beyond production: artist branding, release strategy, and the moves that turn skill into momentum.

STEP 1 Build Your Foundation

Master production fundamentals, music theory and Ableton, and build a workflow you can rely on from day one.

STEP 2 Create Your Sound

Develop your own identity through sound design, synthesis and sampling.

STEP 3 Turn Ideas into Tracks

Turn loops into finished songs with professional arrangement and energy flow.

STEP 4 Finish Like a Pro

Polish with mixing and mastering, then get your tracks release-ready and start building your career.
