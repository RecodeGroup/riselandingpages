Who this is for

You know you want this. You just haven't found the structure yet.

You've been producing for a while. You know your DAW. You've watched the tutorials, bought the plugins, maybe even released a track or two. But most of your projects sit at 80% — almost done, never finished.

You don't need more information. You need direction, honest feedback, and a reason to show up every week. Base gives you the structure, the accountability, and the people who are on the same path — so you stop spinning your wheels and start building a real body of work.
