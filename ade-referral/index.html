    Who this is for

    A foundation you can build the rest of your career on.

    Your drive isn't the problem, your consistency is. Base is where you start finishing tracks instead of abandoning them halfway, find a sound that's your own, and build a workflow you can rely on every time you open your DAW.

    You're not doing it alone. You move through the same six months alongside a small group of producers on exactly the same track. By the end, your sound has matured into something you can actually release.
