The program

What the six months actually include.

A new cohort starts every six months, and you can sign up months ahead. Inside, it's 30 sessions: most of them hands-on production work, plus a handful that step outside production entirely to focus on what actually moves your career forward, working on your artist profile so you can get further with it. Everything is built around the same six months, the same group, the same standard.
