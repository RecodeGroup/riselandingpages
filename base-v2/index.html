Who this is for

A production foundation you can build the rest of your career on.

Your drive isn't the problem, your production is. Base is where you start finishing tracks instead of abandoning them halfway, find a sound that's actually yours, and build a workflow you can rely on every time you sit down to work.

You're not doing it alone, you're doing it alongside other producers on the same six-month track. By the end, your sound has matured into something you can build an actual career on.

The program

What the six months actually include.

A new cohort starts every six months, and you can sign up months ahead. Inside, it's 25 sessions: most of them hands-on production work, plus a handful that step outside production entirely to focus on what actually moves your career forward, working on your artist profile so you can get further with it. Everything is built around the same six months, the same group, the same standard.

Your learning journey

Four steps to becoming a professional producer

STEP 1 Build Your Foundation

Learn production fundamentals, music theory, and Ableton. Build a solid workflow from day one.

STEP 2 Create Your Sound

Develop your sonic identity through sound design, synthesis, and sampling techniques.

STEP 3 Turn Ideas into Tracks

Transform loops into complete songs with professional arrangement and energy flow.

STEP 4 Finish Like a Pro

Polish with mixing and mastering, then prepare your tracks for release and build your career.

Six months pass either way. Spend them building, not waiting.

You already know your production isn't where it needs to be. Waiting doesn't change that, it just delays it. This cohort is where you close the gap.
