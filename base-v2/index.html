Who this is for

A production foundation you can build the rest of your career on.

Your drive isn't the problem, your consistency is. Base is where you start finishing tracks instead of abandoning them halfway, find a sound that's yours, and build a workflow you can rely on every time you sit down to work.

You're not doing it alone, you're doing it alongside other producers on the same six-month track. By the end, your sound has matured into something you can build a career on.

The program

What the six months include.

A new cohort starts every six months, and you can sign up months ahead. Inside, it's 30 sessions: most of them hands-on production work, plus a handful that step outside production entirely to focus on what moves your career forward, working on your artist profile so you can get further with it. Everything is built around the same six months, the same group, the same standard.

Production & Arrangement

Build your foundation in music theory, Ableton workflow, and learn to turn loops into complete tracks

Sound Design & Synthesis

Develop your identity through sound design, synthesis en sampling techniques

Mixing & Mastering

Polish your tracks with professional mixing and mastering techniques to make them release-ready

Career Development

Build your artist profile, plan releases, and learn what it takes to turn music into a sustainable career
