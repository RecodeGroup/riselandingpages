Who it's for

Build the foundation your career deserves.

You don't need more ideas. You need a stronger foundation. Base is where you learn to finish tracks, build better production habits and develop the consistency every producer needs.

You'll do it alongside a cohort of producers who are working towards the same goal: becoming ready for the next step.

The program

What the six months include.

30 Live Lessons

Every week, we host hands-on production sessions that follow our structured lesson curriculum. Together, we cover every essential aspect of music production, including sampling, synthesis, arrangement, melody, harmony, mixing, and production techniques.

Cohort Model

Learn alongside the same group for six months, creating accountability, consistency, and a high-performance environment where everyone progresses together.

Live Coaching

Receive weekly live feedback on your music, ask questions in real time, solve production challenges, and get actionable advice you can immediately apply to your tracks.

Platform Access

Access our complete learning platform with in-depth video modules, premium sample packs, Ableton racks, project files, templates, and production tools that support your progress between coaching sessions.
