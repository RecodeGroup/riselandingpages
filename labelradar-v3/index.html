About this partnership

Label Radar + Rise

Label Radar connects artists directly with labels like Armada Music, Monstercat, NCS, and Toolroom Records. Rise Artist Academy helps artists build sustainable careers. Together, we make sure you do not just submit demos, but submit the right demos to the right labels with a real strategy behind it.

What is Label Radar?

Label Radar is part of the Beatport ecosystem and gives artists a direct line to labels accepting demos. With features like remix contests, an artist network for collaborations, and an artist watchlist, it is the most direct way to get your music in front of decision makers. Members with a Pro subscription or higher qualify for this free strategy call with Rise.
