The weekly group lessons are the heart of BASE. On top of that, you get 7 video modules, sample packs, and production guides covering everything from music theory to mastering. Here's what the program covers:

Music Theory & Foundations

Understand keys, scales, chord progressions, and rhythm. Build the musical vocabulary that separates producers from artists.

  • Keys & scales
  • Chord progressions
  • Rhythm & groove

Producing in Ableton

Master your DAW inside and out. From session view to arrangement, learn the workflows that professional producers use daily.

  • Session & arrangement view
  • MIDI & audio workflows

Sound Design & Synthesis

Create your own sounds from scratch. Learn synthesis, sampling, and processing to develop a signature sonic palette.

  • Subtractive & FM synthesis
  • Serum & Ableton instruments
  • Sampling & resampling
  • Processing & layering

Arrangement & Finishing

Turn loops into complete tracks. Learn arrangement techniques, transitions, builds, and how to actually finish songs.

  • Loop to arrangement
  • Transitions & builds
  • Energy flow & dynamics
  • Finishing & exporting

Mixing & Mastering

Get your tracks sounding polished and release-ready. From EQ and compression to final master chain.

  • EQ, compression & effects
  • Gain staging & balance
  • Spatial mixing & stereo
  • Mastering fundamentals

Acoustics & Home Studio

Optimize your listening environment. Learn room treatment basics and monitoring so your mixes translate everywhere.

  • Room acoustics basics
  • Monitor placement
  • Acoustic treatment
  • Reference listening

Music Industry Basics

Understand how the industry works. Distribution, royalties, branding, and building your first releases.

  • Distribution & releasing
  • Royalties & rights
  • Artist branding
  • Industry landscape
