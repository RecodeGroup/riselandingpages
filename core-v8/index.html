The Industry Code

Your route from producer to booked artist.

A three-phase plan to work towards a festival booking or three gigs in your first 90 days.

PHASE 1 Your sound and story

Define your sound, your positioning and the line-ups you fit.

PHASE 2 Your artist profile

Build your photos, bio, socials, your three strongest tracks and a mix that shows how you play.

PHASE 3 Getting booked

Find the right promoters, venues and festivals, build your press kit, and write personal outreach and follow-ups with feedback from Rise.

You work on your personal artist development plan every week. After the first 90 days you keep going for the rest of the year: releasing, playing and building your network on a steady rhythm.
