The idea

Talent was never the issue.

You can work hard on your craft and still stay in the same place without direction. What's actually missing is two things: a mirror that shows you exactly where you stand, and a structure that won't let you walk away once you see it.

We ran into that same wall for years, across releasing music, playing shows, running labels and building events. What changed things wasn't more information. It was direction, structure, accountability, and an environment that holds a higher standard than you'd hold yourself to.

You don't rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your standards.
