What happens next

We take it from here.

Every application gets personal attention. You'll hear from us within 48 hours on working days. Here's exactly what you can expect.

1

We listen to your demo

Our team listens to the music you submitted, reviews your experience, your goals and where you currently stand as a producer.

2

We schedule an intro call

Before we decide on a tier, we schedule a short introductory call with you. We want to get to know you, understand your ambitions and see if you have the focus and commitment to get the most out of Rise.

3

We determine your level

Based on your demo, your experience, the intro call and your goals, we determine whether you're the best fit for BASE, CORE or ELITE. This way, you're always in the right group.

4

You get started

Once everything is confirmed, you receive access to Rise and can start your journey as an artist right away.
