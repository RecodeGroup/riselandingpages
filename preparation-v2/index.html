Is this a sales call?

It's a conversation. We get to know your goals and give you our honest view on your next step. If a program fits, we'll show you how it works. If not, we'll tell you what we'd do instead.

TEAM · Geen druk, wat er op de call gebeurtTeamlid dat de calls doet, 45 sec. Kern: geen harde verkoop, geen verplichtingen. Je krijgt hoe dan ook richting van iemand die de industrie kent. Leg de opbouw van de call uit in 3 stappen.
Video placeholderHere: a short video about base vs core, voor wie welk programma is.

Do I need to finish tracks already?

No. Base takes you from loops to finished, release-ready tracks. Core is for producers who already finish tracks and want to grow as an artist.

TEAM · Base vs Core, voor wie welk programma isCoach, 45–60 sec. Beschrijf de twee startpunten (alleen loops vs. al hele tracks) en welk programma daarbij past. Eventueel kort scherm met een track van een member.
Video placeholderHere: a short video about investering en tijd per week.

What does it cost, and how much time does it take?

Base is €1,500 and Core starts from €2,650 (excl. VAT), both with payment plans. We go through time and budget together on the call.

TEAM · Investering en tijd per weekMaurits, 45 sec. Wees open over prijs en tijd, zodat er geen verrassingen zijn op de call. Benoem betalingsplannen. Frame het als investering in je carrière, zonder druk.
Video placeholderHere: a short video about wat als het niet werkt / garantie.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Fair question, especially if you've tried courses before. Both programs come with a guarantee. We go through the full conditions on your call.

TEAM · Wat als het niet werkt / garantieMaurits, 45–60 sec. Erken dat veel artiesten al cursussen hebben geprobeerd. Leg uit waarom Rise anders is (feedback, community, labels) en noem de garanties van Base en Core.

How Rise works

Built by artists who release and play internationally.

Rise is the artist academy of Recode Group. Next to Rise, we run four record labels and our own artist agency.

Recode Records Recode Deep Revoke Rex Amplify Agency
Base · 6 months

From a loop to a finished track.

Goal: three full, release-ready tracks within 6 months.
  • 30 live lessons in a small group: 25 on production, 5 on artist development
  • A weekly 3-hour live class, plus homework on your own tracks
  • Direct feedback on your music from coaches who release and play out
  • Expect around 8 hours a week: the class plus homework
€1,500 excl. VAT · Payment plans available
Core · 12 months

From producer to artist.

Goal: working towards 3 gigs or a festival booking within 90 days.
  • Your branding, goals and the concrete steps to get there
  • Two group check-ins a week that hold you to your goals
  • Feedback on releases, bookings and your next steps
  • Remote Sessions writing camps with our labels and agencies, plus masterclasses and the community
From €2,650 excl. VAT · Payment plans available

Results are goals we work towards together, not guarantees. They depend on the time and energy you put in.
